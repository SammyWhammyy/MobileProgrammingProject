# Building C map verification

The three supplied photographs correspond to floors 1, 2, and 3. Only Building C
is included. Each floor now has a separate rectified trace rather than a shared
schematic grid. The first floor preserves the curved auditorium block. Corridor
areas, room outlines, and explicit door approaches use the same coordinate frame.
Walk edges are orthogonal and split at corridor crossings and door approaches.
Coordinates retain the existing widthM and heightM names for compatibility but
represent drawing units, not surveyed measurements. Door access still needs field
verification where the reference is unclear.

Review before public use:

- Confirm all room entrances and corridor access against the building.
- Confirm the same north and south internal staircases connect adjacent floors.
- Confirm which entrance is used on floor 1; main campus entrance is not assumed
  to be the south Building C entrance.
- C304-305 is one combined label in the photograph and remains one searchable
  place with C304 and C305 search tags until the internal division is verified.
- C323 appears nested inside another space on floor 3. It is labelled on the map
  but intentionally not routable until its access door is confirmed.
- Floor 1 IT Helpdesk and service room extents need refinement from a clearer
  reference. The current trace prioritizes the connected corridor topology.
- The lift is not included as a traversable connector until access is verified.
- Unlabelled rooms, WC entrances, external spiral stairs, and the S and N wings
  are outside routable coverage. Do not infer missing rooms from number gaps.
- Verify route overlays visually, particularly near room boundaries. These
  schematic plans are a development MVP and are not evacuation guidance.

Do not show meter distances or walking times until scale and route measurements
have been checked. Record field results with date, route, observation, and fix.
